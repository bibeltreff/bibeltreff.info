---
name: Anonymous
headline: I have tasted how surpassingly excellent Christ is
intro: Freed from a worldly, sinful Christian life – to live with Christ every day.
---

I received the Lord Jesus into my life when I was six years old. He put joy in my heart. My parents told me a lot about the Bible, and I enjoyed going to church.

As a teenager, I developed more and more other interests. Much of it was not wrong in itself or at first: handball, football, friends, having fun … But at some point, these interests began to compete with God without me noticing.

Along with these many interests, more and more sinful things entered my life. This hardened my heart towards God. I attended church less often, had less fellowship, and read the Bible less.

I kept going on Christian retreats, which stirred my heart for God anew each time. I resolved to change my life. But I did not know how to stay connected with God in a lasting way. So I soon ended up back in my old, worldly, and ultimately sinful life.

During church services, I was convicted again and again and promised God that I would change my life. But it never lasted long. Soon I was doing all the same worldly things as my schoolmates again. This seemingly endless cycle of good intentions and quickly falling back into old ways left me dissatisfied, and so I turned away from God more and more.

I thank God that He did not give up on me, even though I deserved it. He brought me among people who truly wanted to follow Jesus. They gave me a taste of a life with and for God. I realised that this life is so much better than anything else. Someone showed me this verse:

> … I also count all things loss for the excellence of the knowledge of Christ Jesus my Lord … that I may gain Christ.
> — Philippians 3:8

During my studies, I met the brothers and sisters from Bibeltreff. Through fellowship with them, God showed me more and more how I could actually live with Him every day.

I learned that the Bible has been given to us as our daily food! God's Word tastes wonderful and strengthens me to overcome! Regularly sharing with the others and praying together also gave me more and more strength to leave my old, worldly life behind. It is so much better to live through and with Christ.

Through further fellowship, the Lord continued to show me that if I want to live with Him consistently and experience Him, I need to learn to serve Him as a priest. In this way, I came to experience how to gain Christ as the spiritual sacrifices in order to live a life pleasing to God. I am endlessly grateful to God for His grace and for the revelation He gives when we seek Him!
